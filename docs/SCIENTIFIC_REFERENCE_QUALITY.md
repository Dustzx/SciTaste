# Content-grounded Scientific Reference quality

Status: implemented at the typed proposal, deterministic verification,
content-free qualification, human-admission, and evidence-program boundaries.
No acquired source body, provider response, human judgment, or effectiveness
result is reported here.

## Why venue retrieval is not Scientific Taste

A prestigious venue, a high citation count, or a successful final result can be
useful discovery priors. None proves that a record exposes the scientific
decision that produced the result. A source cannot teach transferable Taste if
the available material hides the competing actions, the evidence that separated
them, or the conditions under which the selected action would fail.

SciTaste therefore separates three operations:

1. **Discovery** constructs a broad, rights-compatible source pool. Search,
   citations, venues, and metadata are permitted here as recall aids.
2. **Reference qualification** sees a prestige-blind content projection and
   tests whether the source contains a complete contrastive decision episode.
3. **Taste abstraction** distills an admitted episode into a grounded principle
   with explicit applicability and failure boundaries.

The retrieval mechanism is an efficiency layer. Content-grounded qualification
and abstraction are the Scientific Taste method.

## Five-dimensional qualification

The proposal-only `reference-quality` node must assess exactly five properties:

- **Evidential rigor:** decision-relevant evidence is connected to a
  justification, limitation, or observable outcome.
- **Decision traceability:** a scientific action is connected to evidence or a
  justification, rather than inferred from the final paper's success.
- **Alternative visibility:** both a selected action and a genuine alternative
  are visible.
- **Failure-boundary visibility:** the record exposes a limitation and the
  evidence state in which the action ceases to be useful.
- **Transfer potential:** the action is linked to a reusable problem or evidence
  pattern, not merely a source-specific recipe.

Each strong rating must cite exact excerpts and compatible semantic fields.
Qualification requires all five dimensions to be strong and a complete episode
containing alternatives, action, evidence, and limitations. A missing dimension
is a valid rejection rather than an invitation to hallucinate support.

Author, venue, citation count, experimental relation, and downstream task
identity are excluded from the node input. Outcome fields can also be withheld.
The deterministic validator checks source and screening identities, projection
hashes, exact excerpts, field roles, and the five-dimensional closure. The model
cannot admit a source.

## Human and provenance boundary

An accepted live node ledger can be compiled into a content-free qualification
receipt. The receipt retains source, projection, proposal, provider, model, and
ledger hashes but embeds no source text and grants no admission or execution
authority.

Source-admission schema v1.1 requires that receipt, its bound blind projection,
and exactly two independent human reviews. Each reviewer must assess the same
five dimensions, be blind to prestige signals, the model assessment, other
reviews, and downstream outcomes, and bind the exact visible projection hash.
The source curator cannot review their own source. Rights and source-group
isolation remain independent gates. Rejections remain in the frozen population.

This division supports scalable model proposals while retaining a calibrated,
auditable human standard for the formal corpus. It does not treat model-human
agreement as proof that the resulting Taste improves research.

## H0 causal obligation

The ICLR evidence program now distinguishes source quality from abstraction and
selection:

- **H0** compares content-grounded reference admission with prestige-only
  selection from the identical frozen broad pool at equal source count,
  abstraction model, prompt, downstream model, tools, and context budget.
- **H1** holds admitted sources fixed and compares grounded Taste abstraction
  with same-source raw RAG.
- **H2a** tests matched versus source-disjoint mismatched Taste.
- **H2b** tests decision-grounded deliberation versus lexical selection from the
  same hard-negative Taste pool.

H0's primary endpoint is paired, condition-blinded expert preference over the
downstream scientific action and claim calibration. Source qualification
precision, reviewer agreement, dimension failures, and cost are diagnostics.
H0 shares the same held-out decision cases and compatible downstream runs with
H1--H2b; it is not a request for arbitrary seed multiplication.

## Remaining evidence

The method contract is executable, but the claim remains open. Real progress
now requires separately authorized bounded content projection of acquired
sources, real model proposals, two conflict-cleared reviewers, a frozen
prestige-only comparator, source-group-disjoint held-out cases, and powered
outcome analysis. Until then, SciTaste has a falsifiable definition of a
high-quality scientific reference, not evidence that the definition improves
research.
