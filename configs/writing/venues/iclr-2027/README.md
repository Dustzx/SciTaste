# ICLR 2027 venue bundle

This directory keeps three independently versioned contracts together:

- `submission.yaml` binds the official template and deterministic submission rules;
- `taste.yaml` supplies provenance-bearing, advisory venue guidance; and
- `provenance.yaml` records which official criteria and reference observations were
  distilled, together with the limits on their use.

Submission eligibility is determined only by `submission.yaml` and the compiled
artifact checks. `taste.yaml` cannot establish paper quality, predict acceptance, or
override scientific integrity. Corpus-derived principles remain candidates until the
registered comparison populations and independent annotations are complete.
